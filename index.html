Projects

New Alamein is a new fourth generation city in northwestern Egypt inaugurated by Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi on 1 March 2018.

President El-Sisi gave directives to expedite the implementation of targeted projects in the Suez Canal, with particular focus on logistical zones.

Benban Solar Park is a photovoltaic power station in Egypt with a total capacity of 1650 MW nominal power which corresponds to an annual production of approximately 3.8 TWh.

The Grand Egyptian Museum

The world's largest museum of ancient Egyptian antiquities. Located a few miles west of Cairo , it overlooks the Giza Pyramids . The museum houses over 100,000 artifacts from the ancient Egyptian , Greek , and Roman periods , as well as commercial and entertainment facilities, a restoration center, and a museum garden. It was designed to accommodate 5 million visitors annually. Egypt launched an international campaign to fund the museum's construction, with the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) providing soft loans as a major contributor. The museum officially opened on November 1, 2015 , and is considered a major tourist destination contributing to the growth of Egypt's tourism sector .
